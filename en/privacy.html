Privacy policy

Information on ADTC's direct contact channels and the use of optional web analytics on adtc.kz.

Updated: 6 October 2026

Direct inquiries

The current website does not send contact details through a server-side form. You can contact ADTC by phone, WhatsApp, WeChat or email. After you follow these links, communication takes place in the service you choose and is governed by its settings and policies.

Information visitors share

Visitors decide what information to share in a call or correspondence. Digital signatures, passwords, bank details and complete confidential documents are not required for an initial inquiry.

How inquiries are used

Information received through your chosen contact channel is used to respond, clarify the task and continue business correspondence. The legal entity, contractor and processing terms are determined when discussing a specific service or project.

Optional technical analytics

Google Analytics 4 and Yandex Metrica load only after you select 'Allow' in the notice. Analytics receives technical events, including page language and contact clicks, the page address and permitted UTM tags. The website does not send call or correspondence content, names, phone numbers or email addresses to analytics. Declining does not prevent you from using the website or direct contact channels.

Managing your choice

Your analytics choice is saved in your browser. You can change it using the Analytics settings link in the footer. Clearing the website's data in your browser settings also resets the choice, and the notice will appear again.

Visitor requests

To clarify processing procedures, correct information you previously shared or request its deletion, contact us at info@adtc.kz or by phone +7 707 780 7804.

This document describes how the current website actually works and does not claim that a legal review has been completed.
