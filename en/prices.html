Ways to work with us

Tender support options

ADTC offers one-off support, assistance with a single bid or ongoing support. The specific scope and terms are defined after an initial task review.

Ways to work with us

Start with a specific task

One procurement, an application package or ongoing participation — the support scope follows your needs.

Review of a specific tender

When it fits
You need to assess participation and understand the requirements before preparing an application.
What you receive
Key requirements, risks and missing information, with a sequence of next steps.
What to send
The procurement link, submission deadline and your question.
Send a tender for review

Application support

When it fits
A procurement has been selected and the document package needs preparation and review.
What you receive
A document matrix, review comments and preparation and submission checkpoints within the agreed scope.
What to send
The link, submission deadline and a list of documents already available.
Discuss application preparation

Ongoing support

When it fits
Your company regularly participates in procurement and needs a consistent workflow.
What you receive
An agreed list of procurements and tasks, owners and deadlines, with a clear working process.
What to send
Main procurement platforms, supply categories and upcoming procurements.
Discuss ongoing support

Before work starts, we agree the deliverables, timing, fee and responsibilities. We do not promise a tender win.

What determines the scope of work

Urgency

The submission deadline determines the time available for checks, clarification and preparation.

Platform and requirements

The task depends on the procedure, buyer's requirements and document set.

Supply

If product sourcing, logistics or imports are needed, the supply scope is agreed separately.
