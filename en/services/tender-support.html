Service scope

What the work includes

Participation decision

We review the subject, deadlines, requirements, restrictions and initial questions about project economics.

Document matrix

We link each procurement requirement to a document, responsible person and readiness deadline.

Preparation and submission

We check completeness, formats and the final bid version before upload.

Subsequent stages

We track decision reports and agreed actions for auctions, contracting and performance.

What the client receives

  • a list of requirements and findings on the initial data;
  • a bid preparation plan with key deadlines;
  • a checked document set within the agreed scope;
  • clarity on the next actions after the decision report.
Important

ADTC does not guarantee eligibility or a win: the buyer makes the decision based on the participant's actual documents. We are responsible for the agreed scope of analysis and preparation.
