Advice for suppliers

KZTIN / NTIN / NKT for public procurement acceptance certificates

We help assess situations where a product cannot be found in the National Product Catalog, the system requires KZTIN or NTIN, a TRU code does not match a product record, or a delivery note, acceptance certificate or contract cannot be prepared.

Describe the issueOfficial portal guidance

When support is needed

We identify the exact scenario first

Client inquiries use variants such as KZTIN, КЗТИН, NTIN, НТИН, NKT and 'National Product Catalog.' What matters for the review is not the spelling, but the product, document and exact system message.

Acceptance certificate or delivery note cannot be completed

We identify the contract performance stage at which the document stopped being accepted and the product data already entered.

Product not found in NKT

We check the search query, category, characteristics and existing records in the National Product Catalog.

The system requires KZTIN / NTIN

We compare existing codes with the product and the document the supplier is preparing.

TRU code issue

We check the procurement's TRU or OKTRU code against the specification, product record and error text.

A product record is needed

We gather the name, intended use, model, manufacturer, unit of measurement and key characteristics.

A support request is needed

We prepare a focused inquiry for the system operator, buyer or technical support without unnecessary personal data.

What we do

Reviewing codes, records and next steps

The consultation provides a clear next step and a list of data to correct, supplement or send to the responsible participant in the process.

KZTIN / NTIN code selection

We assess the product's intended use and characteristics, existing codes and their connection to the specification. We do not substitute a random similar code.

NKT review

We check search results, categories, product attributes and the status of an existing record.

Error analysis

We compare the exact error text with the document preparation stage, contract and product data.

Product record

We help structure the description and list the characteristics that need supporting evidence.

Inquiry text

We explain exactly what is not working, which actions have been taken and what outcome is needed.

Action plan

We define the sequence of checks and inquiries. Registration and data acceptance decisions remain with the relevant system and organizations.

What to send

Data for a consultation

  • Exact product name and intended use.
  • Model, manufacturer, unit of measurement and main characteristics.
  • TRU / OKTRU code and technical specification, if available.
  • Existing KZTIN or NTIN codes and details of the NKT product record.
  • Document and stage: delivery note, acceptance certificate, contract or another scenario.
  • Full error text or an anonymized screenshot, and the document preparation deadline.
Do not send sensitive information

Digital signatures, passwords, bank details and complete confidential documents are not needed for an initial review.
