Our process

From deadlines to evidence

  1. Check the deadline and procedure. Identify the applicable appeal procedure and the time remaining.
  2. Compare the grounds. Check the decision report against the procurement requirements and submitted bid.
  3. Separate facts from assumptions. Gather documents and references supporting the supplier's position.
  4. Structure the arguments. State the specific violation, supporting evidence and requested action.
  5. Define the next step. Explain the procedure after submission or receipt of a response.

Review outcome

You receive an assessment of the appeal's prospects, a list of evidence and a prepared appeal text within the agreed scope. If the grounds are insufficient or the deadline has passed, we tell you directly.
