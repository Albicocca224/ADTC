Procurement and international supply

Manufacturer sourcing and supply coordination

We find manufacturers to meet the technical specification, compare offers and establish the specifications, timing, documents and supply route in advance.

Discuss your supply project

We work with China, Europe and other markets. Customs clearance and transport are handled by specialist contractors under an agreed arrangement.

Capabilities

From technical specification to delivery

Factory and manufacturer sourcing

We source manufacturers for the task, request commercial offers and compare prices, lead times, minimum order quantities and payment terms.

Compliance review

We compare models and characteristics with the technical specification, request documents and note discrepancies before the order is agreed.

Negotiations and order preparation

We clarify commercial terms, included components, packaging, production lead times and coordination with the selected manufacturer.

Logistics and documents

We organize international logistics and supply documentation with specialist carriers, brokers and other contractors.

Work stages

  1. Receive your task. Product, quantity, technical requirements, city, deadline and indicative budget.
  2. Review the market. Gather comparable offers and questions about the specification.
  3. Agree on an option. Confirm the manufacturer, specifications, price, documents and commercial terms.
  4. Plan the supply. Define the route, checkpoints and responsible participants.
  5. Support delivery. Monitor the agreed stages and document set within the contract's scope.
Service boundaries

ADTC does not claim customs representative status. Customs clearance and transport are handled by the relevant engaged contractors where the agreed arrangement provides for them.

What to send for an initial estimate

  • technical specification or product list;
  • quantity and mandatory characteristics;
  • city and desired delivery date;
  • indicative budget, if available;
  • procurement link if the supply is tender-related.

Even if some data is missing, we will identify what needs clarification before contacting manufacturers.
