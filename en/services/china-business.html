For Chinese manufacturers: tenders and supply projects in Kazakhstan

We help align a manufacturer's offer with the requirements of a specific project in Kazakhstan: equipment, documentation, commercial terms and supply coordination.

Discuss the project

To get started, send a catalog or equipment model and a brief task description. For a specific procurement, include the link and submission deadline.

Focused support

What ADTC can handle

The scope is defined by the product, procurement procedure and each participant's role.

Procurement requirements review

We review the technical specification, document set and deadlines. Before preparing a bid, we identify questions about participation terms and potential restrictions.

Matching equipment to the specification

We compare the model and characteristics, record discrepancies and request clarification from the manufacturer. We prepare a compliance table for the project discussion.

Supply project coordination

We clarify included components, the commercial offer, production lead times, documents and route. We coordinate with supply participants under an agreed plan.

How to get started

From a catalog to a specific project review

  1. Product introduction. Catalog, model, specifications, intended use and responsible person's contact details.
  2. Task review. Procurement or buyer inquiry, deadline and required initial data.
  3. Agreeing the approach. Scope, deliverables, participants and commercial terms.
  4. Stage-by-stage work. Documents, technical clarification and coordination of agreed actions.

Practical experience

ADTC founder Bekzat Aidar has more than 6 years of experience in tenders, procurement and supply project support. The team's experience includes working with manufacturers in China and Europe and supplying specialized equipment.

About the company and our approach

Roles and responsibilities

Participation options and requirements are checked for each specific procurement. ADTC does not promise a win, sales volumes or exclusive market rights.

Transport, customs clearance and special permits are handled by the relevant specialist participants. Their roles and responsibilities are defined separately.

For certain projects, Eurasia Line LLP, a separate legal entity, may act as the contractor. Its documents and licenses are not ADTC's licenses. The contractor and contractual arrangement are defined for each project.
