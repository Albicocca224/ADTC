About us

Adex Trade Company helps businesses take part in procurement with greater confidence

Adex Trade Company is a team working in tender support, procurement and supply coordination. We assess projects from the buyer's requirements through supplier selection, project economics and delivery of contractual obligations.

Founder

Bekzat Aidar

Founder of Adex Trade Company (ADTC) and CEO of Eurasia Line LLP.

He has more than 6 years of experience in tenders, procurement and supply project support. His practical experience includes reviewing tender documents and technical specifications, preparing bids, working with manufacturers in China and Europe, international logistics, import coordination and supplying specialized equipment.

The team delivers projects with specialist manufacturers, suppliers, logistics providers and other contractors, depending on the task.

Separate legal entities

Each company has its own role and responsibilities

ADTC and Eurasia Line LLP are not the same legal entity. For certain projects, Eurasia Line LLP may act as the contractor: it is a separate company with its own documents and licenses. The legal entity, terms and responsibilities are defined separately for each project.
